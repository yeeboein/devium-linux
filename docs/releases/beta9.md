# Devium beta9

A fresh look: a **centred start menu** and the new **Devium icon pack**, plus a smoother Devium Setup.

## New look (Devium and Devium Dark themes)
- **Start menu in the middle**: the start button and your apps sit in the centre of the big floating
  bar, so the menu opens from the middle of the screen. Tray and clock move to the right
  (with ArchPill on, it's just the start button and apps).
- **Devium icon pack**: every app icon is a rounded square in the Devium gradient with the app's
  logo in white - Firefox, Brave, VS Code, Steam, Discord, Spotify, LibreOffice, GIMP and many more.
- Base KDE keeps KDE's normal panel and Breeze icons.

## Devium Setup
- The progress bar now moves smoothly during each step (downloads, installs, Flathub apps, AI model)
  and shows a percentage, instead of jumping from 0% to 50% to 100%.

## Smaller download
- The KDE Plasma ISO is **one file** (about 1.9 GB). To fit, the ISO carries translations for English,
  Irish, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish and Russian; other languages come
  back with your first update.

## Also in Devium (since beta8)
- Devium Tools (Meta+T): App Manager, Safe Updates with undo, Make it faster, Low-RAM mode, battery,
  privacy, printers, Docker/Podman, Git and GitHub, Coming from Windows, accessibility, languages,
  Report a problem
- Optional Local AI, Magnify dock, disk encryption, `support fix`, Join the Devium Discord
