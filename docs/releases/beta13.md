# Devium beta13

**New: a Hyprland edition!** Three ISOs now: KDE Plasma, Xfce and Hyprland.

## Hyprland edition (new)
- A tiling desktop in the Devium colours: windows arrange themselves, with soft corners and a gradient border.
- Waybar on top with the Devium logo as the start button, fuzzel to open apps, mako notifications,
  hyprlock lock screen, kitty terminal and Thunar for files.
- Beginner-friendly shortcuts: SUPER+Space apps, SUPER+Return terminal, SUPER+Q close, SUPER+T Devium Tools.
  **SUPER + /** shows every shortcut.
- Same installer, Devium Configure and Devium Tools as the other editions.

## Dock
- **The Devium start button comes first, all your apps behind it** (start | apps | tray). A login check keeps it
  that way, even after a restart.

## Linux Magnify (magdoc)
- Uses your icon pack (the Devium icons by default), and follows you if you change it.
- New **Turn Off magdoc** button in its settings, to get the Devium dock back without uninstalling.
- **Uninstalling it in Devium Configure puts back your exact look** from before you turned it on: dock, panels,
  colours, icons, theme and window style.

## Languages
- **Hungarian (Magyar):** About Devium, the install splash and the credits are translated, and KDE's own Hungarian
  is now on the ISO (thanks to our community translator!).

## Fixed
- Devium Configure no longer crashes if KDE's colour settings file is damaged.

## Tip for VirtualBox
Turn on **EFI**, give it **20 GB** of disk and **4 GB** of RAM, and remove the ISO after installing.
