# Devium beta10

A Mac-like polish pass: a dock that fits its apps, pastel window buttons, an ArchPill tour,
a "Terminal fun" page, smaller installs and no more install loop.

## Look
- **Dock fits its apps**: a floating dock in the middle, exactly as wide as its icons. The terminal is pinned.
- **Pastel macOS-style window buttons**: close, minimise and maximise on the left of every window
  (Devium and Devium Dark themes; Base KDE keeps KDE's buttons).
- **Devium icon pack** and the Devium themes, as in beta9.

## Devium Setup
- **Full KDE or KDE Lite is recommended for your RAM** (8 GB or more: Full KDE).
- **Terminal fun** page: cmatrix, cbonsai, asciiquarium, sl, cowsay, figlet + lolcat, tldr, bat, eza,
  fzf + zoxide, tmux, ncdu, htop, ranger.
- **Open it again any time**: "Let's configure your Devium", everything you have is already ticked,
  and it updates instead of installing.
- **ArchPill tour** after setup (you can skip it), then ArchPill starts. ArchPill now takes over
  notifications and the volume popup by itself.
- **"We recommend a restart"** at the end, with Restart now / Ignore.
- **New Linux Magnify**: replaces the dock, copies your pinned apps, has its own settings app.

## Installer
- **Needs only 10 GB** (was 12): no spare boot image, less space held back.
- **No more install loop**: if you start the ISO/USB again after installing, Devium tells you how to start
  your installed system. In BIOS mode the boot menu has **Start installed Devium**.
- A reminder to remove the USB stick or ISO when it's finished.
- Printer support is now one click in Devium Tools instead of always installed.

## Tip for VirtualBox
Turn on **EFI** (Settings > System), give it **20 GB** of disk and **4 GB** of RAM.
