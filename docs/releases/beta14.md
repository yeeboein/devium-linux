# Devium beta14

Three editions: **KDE Plasma**, **Xfce** and **Hyprland**.

## New
- **Wi-Fi first:** the live USB opens with a Wi-Fi screen, so you're online before installing
  (it skips itself if you're already connected).
- **Devium login screen:** the mountains, a big clock, a frosted password card, your own profile picture
  (from System Settings > Users) or the Devium logo, and a desktop picker (KDE and Hyprland editions).
- **Full KDE keeps KDE's full animations** (KDE Lite and Low-RAM mode stay lighter).

## Removed
- **Local AI** is no longer offered in Devium Configure.

## Installer
- If the installer ever crashes, its log is saved to your Desktop (`devium-installer-log.txt`) so you can
  send it to us. We're looking into a crash with "Replace a partition"; until it's fixed, use
  **Erase disk** or **Manual partitioning**.

## Tip for VirtualBox
Turn on **EFI**, give it **20 GB** of disk and **4 GB** of RAM, and remove the ISO after installing.
