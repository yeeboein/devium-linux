## Built on
- [Arch Linux](https://archlinux.org) and [archiso](https://gitlab.archlinux.org/archlinux/archiso). Devium is based
  on Arch Linux but is not an official Arch product and is not made or supported by the Arch Linux team.
- [KDE Plasma](https://kde.org/plasma-desktop), [Xfce](https://xfce.org), [Hyprland](https://hyprland.org)

- [fastfetch](https://github.com/fastfetch-cli/fastfetch) (MIT)
- [Quickshell](https://quickshell.outfoxxed.me) (LGPL-3.0) for desk widgets on Hyprland and ArchPill

## Icons
- **Devium icon theme** (`usr/share/icons/Devium*`): made by `tools/make-icons.py` and `tools/make-folder-icons.py`.
  - App logos are shapes from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) (CC0-1.0). The brands and logos belong to
    their owners and are used only to show which app is which.
  - System app symbols are shapes from KDE's [Breeze icons](https://invent.kde.org/frameworks/breeze-icons)
    (LGPL-3.0-or-later).
  - Folder, file, trash, Dolphin and file manager icons are drawn from scratch in `tools/devium_tiles.py`
    (GPL-3.0, Devium).
- Devium logo and branding (`docs/branding`, `usr/share/devium/logo*.png`): © Yeeboein.

## Artwork
- **Mountain wallpaper, login background and installer picture** (`usr/share/wallpapers/Devium`, SDDM theme,

  in software), with the Devium logo added. *Photographer and photo link: to be added.*

- Theme previews in Devium Configure: drawn by `tools/make-previews.py` on the wallpaper.
- Theme previews and screenshots: Devium.

## Fonts and emoji
Devium uses fonts from the Arch repos (Noto and JetBrains Mono under the SIL Open Font License, and Nerd Font symbols under MIT). Emoji styles in
Devium Configure install Noto Color Emoji (OFL), Twemoji (CC-BY 4.0) or JoyPixels (JoyPixels free licence, which
you accept when picking it). Devium does not ship Apple's emoji font.
